### Why this one

- 🎯 **Real qualities with real sizes.** Every choice (e.g. 720p · ~110 MB,
  1080p 60 fps · 220 MB) comes from the actual video. No fake buttons.
- 📊 **Live progress you can trust.** Waiting → preparing with a real
  percentage → saved, measured on the server, not a fake timer bar.
- 🖼️ **A button on the player and on thumbnails.** Point at the video you're
  watching and press ⬇ Download right on it; on listings, search results and
  profiles, hover a thumbnail and press ⬇ NSFWDL without opening it (desktop).
- ⚡ **One click with a preferred quality.** Pick Best, 1080p, 720p, 480p or
  Smallest once in ⚙ Settings; after that, Download starts it straight away.
- 📥 **Queue as many as you like.** Three run at a time (four with Supporter), the rest wait
  their turn and start on their own. Close the panel and they keep going.
- ✅ **See what you already have.** Videos you saved get a green outline on
  their thumbnails and "✓ Downloaded" on the button (stored only in your
  userscript manager; clear it or turn it off in ⚙ Settings).
- 🔔 **Works in a background tab.** Progress in the tab title, and a
  notification when the file is saved.
- ⌨️ **Alt+Shift+D** opens the downloader. Minimize the button to a dot, left
  or right corner.
- 🚫 **No pop-ups. No redirects. No fake download buttons.** In the script
  and on nsfwdl.com.

### Free and Supporter (disclosed as `@antifeature payment`)

Downloads are **free**, up to 20 a day. More a day (300) and files over 500 MB need a paid
[NSFWDL Supporter key](https://nsfwdl.com/supporter). Those qualities show a
🔒 and are never started for a free session. Have a key? Enter it once in

### Privacy

- Talks **only to nsfwdl.com** (`@connect nsfwdl.com`, `dl.nsfwdl.com`).
- **Nothing is sent until you click.** Then only the video's public page
  link goes to nsfwdl.com to list qualities and prepare the file.
- No analytics, ads or tracking code in the script. It stores only its
  settings and the list of videos you saved (for the ✓ marks), in your
  userscript manager, never sent anywhere.
- Readable, unminified source (MIT):
  [GitHub](https://github.com/samrtulethtb/nsfwdl-download-button).

### Good to know

- Without `GM_xmlhttpRequest` (some managers), the button opens the video in
  NSFWDL in a new tab instead.
- On RedGifs feeds (home, niches, profiles) the button takes the clip on
  screen, even though the page link stays the same while you scroll. On
  xHamster Shorts it takes the clip you're watching, the first one included.
- Private, login-only or paid videos are not supported.
- Download only videos you are allowed to save.
