## 📲 Install and use

- **Desktop:** install [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) or
  [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/), then hit the install
  button on this page. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Opera and Brave.
- **Android:** Firefox with Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey, or Microsoft Edge
  with Tampermonkey. Downloads finish just like on desktop.
- **iPhone / iPad:** Safari with the free **Userscripts** app or Stay. Tell
  us in the Feedback tab how it goes.

On a supported video page a small **⬇ Download** button appears in the
corner. Open the panel, pick a quality that really exists (with its size),
and the MP4 saves itself.

## ✨ What you get

- **Real qualities, real sizes.** Every option (e.g. 720p · ~110 MB,
  1080p 60 fps · 220 MB) comes from the actual video, exact or estimated
  sizes marked "~". No fake download buttons, ever.
- **Live progress, measured on the server.** Waiting → preparing with a real
  percentage → handed to your browser → saved. Not a fake timer bar.
- **A button on the player and on thumbnails** (desktop). Point at the video
  you're watching and press ⬇ Download right on it; hover a thumbnail on a
  listing, search or profile page and press ⬇ NSFWDL without opening it.
- **One click with a preferred quality.** Pick Best, 1080p, 720p, 480p or
  Smallest once in ⚙ Settings; after that, every Download starts it straight
  away.
- **Queue as many as you like.** Three run at a time (four with Supporter);
  the rest wait their turn in the panel and start on their own. Close the
  panel and they keep going.
- **See what you already have.** Saved videos get a green outline and
  "✓ Downloaded" on their thumbnails — stored only in your userscript
  manager, and you can clear them or turn the markers off in ⚙ Settings.
- **Works in a background tab.** The tab title shows the progress
  ("⬇ 45% · …"), and a notification tells you when a file is saved, or when
  it is ready and needs one tap.
- **Alt+Shift+D** opens the downloader on a video page. Minimize the button
  to a small dot, left or right corner — your choice is remembered.

## 🔧 How NSFWDL works — and why limits exist

NSFWDL is more than a browser userscript. The script provides the in-page
interface, while a self-hosted backend run by NSFWDL and powered by
[yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) handles download processing for a
wide range of adult platforms.

Running that backend takes real infrastructure: server capacity, CPU,
memory, temporary storage, electricity and network bandwidth. Those
resources cost money, even when downloads are free.

The Free tier is designed for everyday use. Automated scripts, bots and
heavy bulk downloading can consume substantial resources and affect
availability for everyone else. Reasonable daily limits help keep the
service useful and sustainable.

- **Free:** up to **30 downloads per day**.
- **Supporter:** up to **300 downloads per day** and downloads of files over
  500 MB — up to about 2 GB — subject to the actual implemented rules.

Supporter contributions help cover the ongoing costs of operating and
improving the backend. They also unlock additional capabilities for people
who need them.

No pressure: if Free covers your needs, enjoy it. If you use NSFWDL
regularly and value the convenience, Supporter is a way to help keep the
service running.

## 🆓 Free vs Supporter

- **Free — no account, no sign-up:** 30 downloads a day, 3 downloading at a
  time, files up to about **500 MB**, normal processing. When you're near
  the daily cap, the panel shows how many are left and links to the optional
  [Supporter key](https://nsfwdl.com/supporter) if you want more room.
- **Supporter — a one-time pass:** 300 downloads a day, 4 at a time in the
  script, files up to about **2 GB**, and priority when the shared server is
  busy. No subscription or account is created.
- Enter a key once in ⚙ Settings → Supporter key and the large qualities
  unlock right in the panel. Only a session token is kept (never the key),
  and it goes only to nsfwdl.com.
- **Quality is never locked:** a 1080p file under 500 MB downloads fine on
  Free.

## 🛠 Prefer to run downloads yourself?

[yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) is free, open-source software you
can install and run on your own computer. It lets you manage your own
downloads and use your own system resources without depending on the NSFWDL
backend.

The trade-off: you run the setup yourself, and the sites, formats and
behavior can differ from NSFWDL's. Great if you enjoy that control.

## 🔒 Privacy and permissions

- **It talks only to NSFWDL.** The script's `@connect` list is exactly
  `nsfwdl.com` and `dl.nsfwdl.com`, the two hosts that serve formats and
  files. No analytics, ads or tracking code, and the
  [source is readable and unminified](https://github.com/samrtulethtb/nsfwdl-download-button) (MIT).
- **Nothing is requested until you open the panel.** Browsing, or hovering
  the player and thumbnails, sends nothing. When you open the panel, the
  video's public page link goes to nsfwdl.com to list its real formats;
  choosing one starts the job and the panel follows its status. Requests
  carry a fixed `/userscript` marker so downloads made from this script can
  be counted — and, like any visit, they reach NSFWDL from your IP address.
- **Optional Supporter key.** Userscript managers don't send nsfwdl.com
  cookies, so a key you enter is exchanged once on nsfwdl.com for a session
  token. Only the token is stored (never the key), and it is sent only to
  nsfwdl.com, in an `X-NSFWDL-Supporter` header. "Remove from this browser"
  ends the session.
- **Local-only storage.** Your settings, how you minimized the button and —
  unless you turn it off — the list of videos you saved stay in your
  userscript manager and are never sent anywhere.

## ⚠️ Good to know

- **Managers without `GM_xmlhttpRequest`:** the button opens the video on
  NSFWDL in a new tab instead.
- **RedGifs feeds** (home, niches, profiles): the button picks the clip on
  screen, even though the page link doesn't change while you scroll. On
  xHamster Shorts it picks the clip you're watching, the first one included.
- **Public videos only.** No private, login-only, paid or subscription
  content, no DRM circumvention, no live-cam recording. If a site isn't in
  the list above, the universal downloader is worth a try.
- **Real options only.** If the uploader provided no 1080p, there is no
  1080p to offer — the quality list only shows versions that exist.
- Download only videos you are allowed to save.
